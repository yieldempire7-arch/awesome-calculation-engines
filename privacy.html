Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 16, 2026 • Effective Immediately
Core Privacy Commitment: ToolsVault calculation engines execute 100% locally within your client browser using client-side JavaScript. We never transmit, store, or sell the financial figures, bid estimates, or proprietary project specs you enter into our calculation tools.

1. Overview & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which ToolsVault ("we", "us", or "our"), directed by John Granbridge, handles telemetry, cookies, and user privacy across https://tools-vault-4ml.pages.dev and its associated software suites.

2. Information We Do Not Collect (Client-Side Privacy)

Unlike traditional SaaS estimating platforms that require account creation and store your project financials on cloud servers, ToolsVault operates on a zero-login, zero-retention architecture. All inputs—including commercial real estate cash flows, subcontractor labor rates, solar pro formas, and trench dimensions—are processed ephemeral in your local browser memory. When you close the tab, your input data is cleared.

3. Cookies & Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense Disclosure)

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies and web beacons to collect non-personally identifiable information (such as your browser type, device type, time of visit, and pages visited) to provide relevant advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

  • Third-Party Cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to ToolsVault or other websites on the Internet.
  • Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you may opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Web Analytics & Server Logs

To monitor site performance, prevent DDoS attacks, and understand aggregate usage trends, our hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare Pages) records standard server logs and privacy-preserving Real User Monitoring (Cloudflare Web Analytics). These logs record technical data such as IP addresses (anonymized), page request paths, HTTP response codes, and device form factors. No personally identifiable information (PII) is linked to these metrics.

5. Commercial Communications & Direct Outreach

If you contact our engineering or commercial desk via email (toolsvault78@gmail.com), your email address and correspondence details are used exclusively to answer your inquiry, coordinate commercial territory routing, or assist with digital asset fulfillment. We will never sell, rent, or trade your email address to third-party data brokers.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, California residents have the right to know what personal data is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. ToolsVault does not sell personal information for monetary consideration. You may exercise your rights by contacting us at toolsvault78@gmail.com.

7. European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

For visitors located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, our legal basis for processing non-essential analytics and advertising cookies is user consent or legitimate commercial interests under Article 6 of the GDPR. You may manage your cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings.
